I'm Ondřej Panuška, writing here as Ondrex. Since 2003 I've worked around software and online marketing: first as a product manager and marketer, since 2015 as a freelance performance marketer, and since September 2022 I run paid marketing for the Czech Republic and Slovakia at CEWE Group (search, display, social and app campaigns).

My daily work is budgets, measurement and data: Google Ads, Meta and Microsoft Ads, GA4 and Adobe Analytics, Google Tag Manager, and scripts that watch what would otherwise take hours of manual clicking. I share some of them on this blog.

Outside of work I build games and small apps and enjoy reading about the history of printing and medieval scriptoria, which is how the blog got its name. Opinions on this blog are my own, not my employer's.
