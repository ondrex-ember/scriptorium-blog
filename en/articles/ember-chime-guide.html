Ember Chime - custom time guide

Ondrex

Ember Chime is a gentle hourly chime for Android that helps you pause, take a breath, and return to the present moment – as often as you want. Join the testing.

✦   ✦   ✦

Time often slips through our fingers in the rush of daily work and screen time. Ember Chime was created to softly mark the passage of hours with peaceful bell chimes reminiscent of ancient monastic bells.

Features include adjustable active hours (so it won't disturb your sleep), subtle volume controls, and optional planetary hour calculations based on your location.
