Scriptorium: Czech game about ink, parchment, and survival in a monastery

Ondrex

Olomouc, 1465. You were given a cell, a goose quill, and a debt to the abbot. Cellarium with groschen awaits behind the wall, the first transmutation attempt smolders in the athanor, and an old scribe claims you will run out of ink by morning.

✦   ✦   ✦

Scriptorium is a medieval idle strategy game set in Olomouc in 1465. Play as a monk scribe copying manuscripts, collecting oak galls for ink, trading in the cellarium, and mastering monastery alchemy.

Play free online at myscriptorium.cz.
