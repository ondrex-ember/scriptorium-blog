City of ink and lead: how printing came to Prague

Ondrex

An anonymous figure who feared the scribes' guild. A printer who became mayor. And Melantrich — the man who swallowed the competition and gave his name to an entire street.

✦   ✦   ✦

The introduction of movable type in late 15th-century Prague fundamentally reshaped Czech literature and society. Early workshops operated under secrecy to avoid conflict with established scribes' guilds.

From early anonymous incunabula to Jiří Melantrich's Renaissance publishing powerhouse, Prague became a central hub of Central European typography.
