Ember Chime is officially released:
learn how to estimate time

Ondrex

Have you ever wanted to learn to feel time better and naturally improve your sense of time? Ever wondered how to read its flow beyond fast-moving watch hands? Try Ember Chime on the Google Play Store! It is and always will be completely free and ad-free.

✦   ✦   ✦

After an open beta period, Ember Chime has reached its first full release version on Google Play. If you haven't heard of it yet, the idea is simple: it is a quiet companion that helps you stop for a moment during the day, take a deep breath, and return to the present moment.

Rather than loud alarms, Ember Chime offers subtle sound notifications, planetary hour readings, and customizable time intervals.

Key Features

- Hourly and custom interval chimes
- Gentle acoustic bell sounds
- Planetary hours and lunar cycles
- Clean, distraction-free interface

You can download the app for free directly on Google Play Store.
