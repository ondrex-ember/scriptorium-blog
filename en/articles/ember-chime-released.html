Ember Chime is officially released:
learn how to estimate time

Ondrex

Have you ever wanted to learn to feel time better and naturally improve your sense of time? Ever wondered how to read its flow beyond fast-moving watch hands? Try Ember Chime on the Google Play Store! It is and always will be completely free and ad-free.

✦   ✦   ✦

After an open beta period, the Ember Chime app has reached its first full release version on Google Play. If you haven't heard of it yet, the idea is very simple: it is a quiet companion that helps you stop for a moment during the day, take a deep breath, and return to the present moment.

It doesn't fight for your attention and doesn't send you notifications about what you're missing in the world. It runs quietly in the background, requires no internet connection, and doesn't ask you to create any user accounts. It's simply a neutral tool – like a watch or a compass.

Basic rhythm of the day

The heart of the app is a gentle notification at times you determine yourself. You might be surprised to find that after using the app for a while, you perceive intervals much more accurately. Learn how to estimate time. If that is important to you, of course :).

  • Intervals: You can choose from preset times (from 5 minutes to 2 hours) or set a completely custom interval down to the minute. Regular reminders have a great hidden benefit – after using the app for some time, you will find that your internal compass has become more accurate and you can estimate real time much better even without looking at the display.
  • Sounds: There are five basic tones available (from a gentle "Chime" to "Sanctus" or "Ave Maria"). If they don't suit you, you can upload any custom sound file directly from your phone.
  • Peaceful sleep: The Quiet Hours function ensures that the app fully respects your rest time and will never disturb you at night.
  • Sun: After entering your location (or letting it load automatically), the app precisely calculates the times of sunrise and sunset.

New layers of time perception

Beyond simply measuring minutes, the full version also brings a view of time through the lens of natural and historical cycles.

  • Planetary hours: The app uses the ancient Chaldean system and calculates the ruling planets from sunrise to sunset anew every day. You can thus track which hour is currently passing and what qualities (e.g., strength, creativity, or reflection) are traditionally attributed to it.
  • Lunar calendar: Directly in the app, you will find the current moon phase in percentage of illumination and exact dates of upcoming full moons and new moons for the next 60 days.

Tailored spiritual profiles

We all perceive time a little differently. That's why Ember Chime now offers the option to activate a specific spiritual profile that aligns your day with traditional times of prayer and rituals. All these times are calculated dynamically based on the real movement of the sun in your specific location.

The choices include:

  • Christian profile (Liturgy of the Hours).
  • Islamic profile (daily prayer times).
  • Jewish profile (Halachic zmanim).
  • Celtic profile (eight phases of the day tied to the sun's movement).

The app itself remains fundamentally and absolutely religiously neutral. It doesn't favor anyone, doesn't force a single path, it merely becomes a mirror for what is important to you.

Privacy first

A crucial point in conclusion: Ember Chime collects absolutely no data. Everything you set in the app – your location, sounds, and chosen spiritual practice – remains exclusively on your device. There is no cloud synchronization, no background analytics, and no ads.

Still on the journey

Ember Chime is not a closed chapter. The app is constantly evolving and there are plans to open doors to other spiritual traditions and features - Orthodoxy, Protestant versus Evangelical order, Shiite and Sunni approaches, deepening of Buddhist traditions, but also an enrichment for the "Order of Hens" (Wake up with the hens, fall asleep with the sunset). And what is most important to me – the development is completely open. If you have an idea for an improvement, something is misbehaving in the app, or you are missing a specific feature, you can easily contact me directly anytime (for example via the email listed on Google Play). I will carefully and gladly read every suggestion. The app is currently available for devices with Android 8.0 and newer, fully localized into Czech and English. Learn to perceive time better, in your own way.
