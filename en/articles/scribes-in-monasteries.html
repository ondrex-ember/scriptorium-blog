Three fingers write, the whole body suffers: how scribes lived in medieval monasteries

Ondrex

The typo demon Titivillus, women with teeth stained blue from lapis lazuli pigment, and monks writing complaints in the margins of parchment manuscripts. Real life in the scriptorium was far from quiet.

✦   ✦   ✦

Monastic scribes worked under rigorous physical hardship. Writing in unheated scriptoria with natural sunlight only, scribes often left marginal notes complaining about freezing fingers, poor ink, and aching backs.

"Tres digiti scribunt, totum corpus laborat" — Three fingers write, but the whole body suffers. A fascinating look into medieval manuscript creation.
