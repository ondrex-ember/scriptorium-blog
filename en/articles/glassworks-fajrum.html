Glassworks Fajrum: Czech game about fire, sand and furnace operation

Ondrex

Arnsdorf, 1680. You inherited a furnace, a recipe, and a debt of fifty groschen. Buyers from Haida await behind the forest, the first batch is crackling in the furnace, and your glassblower wants a raise.

✦   ✦   ✦

Glassworks Fajrum is a historical strategy game set in North Bohemia during the late 17th century. Managing a glassworks required balancing rare raw materials (quartz sand, potash, beech wood) with master craftsmanship and delicate financial management.

Try the game online at fajrum.cz.
